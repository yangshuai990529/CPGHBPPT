# Competitor dimensions

Possible dimensions: panel, backlight, local dimming, brightness, color gamut/accuracy, HDR format, motion, upscaling, AI picture, scene detection, environment adaptation, gaming, calibration, picture mode, user and professional control.

AI Picture briefs prioritize scene/content recognition, processing, automatic adjustment, ambient adaptation, personalization and user control. Mini LED briefs prioritize backlight/local dimming, HDR, brightness measurement conditions and motion. Calibration briefs prioritize signal, mode, target, parameters and result validation. None are a vendor capability claim.
