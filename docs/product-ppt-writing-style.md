# Evidence-driven writing (draft guidance)

Prefer conclusion-first titles that name an object and a supported change; avoid “全面赋能用户体验” unless the audience, effect and evidence are explicit. Body copy should distinguish official claim, observed data, interpretation and proposed action. Do not manufacture numerical improvements such as “6 steps to 2” without measured before/after. Use the glossary in `knowledge-base/glossary/terms.json` to keep picture-mode and signal terminology consistent. The writing critic flags vague titles and long copy; it does not rewrite prose automatically.
